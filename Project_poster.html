"Perancangan Tata Letak Fasilitas Pabrik Mainan Pesawat Kayu"
In the manufacturing industry, facility layout design is crucial to supporting the efficiency and effectiveness of the production process. In the toy industry, particularly for wood-based aircraft toys, optimal facility arrangement is important for the smooth flow of materials, minimizing waiting time, and increasing productivity and work safety. With the growing market demand for educational and eco-friendly toys, factory layout planning must accommodate flexible, ergonomic, and space-efficient production processes to meet changing needs. Therefore, designing the layout of a wooden airplane toy factory is a strategic step to ensure efficient, sustainable operations. When designing the layout of factory facilities for manufacturing activities, such as producing wooden airplane toys, three important stages are commonly used to systematically arrange work areas: the Activity Relationship Chart (ARC), the Activity Relationship Diagram (ADD), and the template. The production layout mockup for the wooden airplane toy is designed using a product layout approach because the process flow follows a fixed sequence from raw materials to finished product. The layout aims to minimize material movement, facilitate workflow, and improve production efficiency.
