Industrial Automation Engineering Competency Test (3 Phase)


SMKN 1 Cileungsi
April 2018
Kab Bogor, Indonesia

"Uji Kompetensi Keahlian Teknik Otomasi Industri"
The Certificate of Competency Test of Industrial Automation Engineering is used to prepare vocational students. It provides students with practical knowledge to understand the preparation, process, and installation of three-phase electrical circuits with PLC programs. Created process systematics and work procedures, such as installed panel boxes on demonstration boards and PLC control components on panel doors and trays, assembled control circuits and power wiring. Checked the installation of PLC-based control system components, conducted power circuit tests and control circuit tests and created PLC programming languages. Deliverables: Tested a three-phase motor control panel based on a PLC system.
